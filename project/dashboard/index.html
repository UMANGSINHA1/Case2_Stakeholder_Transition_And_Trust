    Validation and limitations

    Implemented validation
    • Paid claims only; rejected and reversed claims excluded.
    • 12-month continuous look-back and washout.
    • 12-month continuous look-forward for original cohort.
    • Refill-gap boundary tests at 60 and 61 days.
    • Early refill, restart and insurance-loss tests.
    • Wilson 95% confidence interval for fixed six-month proportion.
    Outstanding / interpret carefully
    • Lifelines cross-validation has not run in the build environment; install Lifelines and rerun locally.
    • Kaplan–Meier non-informative censoring assumption may not hold for insurance loss.
    • Stockpiling cap of 30 days is a proposed assumption, not specified by the case.
    • Claims indicate dispensing, not ingestion or adherence.
    • Synthetic data cannot validate real-world patient persistence or the historical 68%.
